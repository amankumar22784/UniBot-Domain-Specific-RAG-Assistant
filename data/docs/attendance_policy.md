# Attendance Policy (SAMPLE - replace with your university's real documents)

Students must maintain a minimum attendance of 75 percent in every course, theory and laboratory combined, to be eligible to sit the end-semester examination. Attendance is calculated up to the last teaching day of the semester.

Students whose attendance falls between 65 and 75 percent may apply for condonation to the Dean of Academic Affairs, with supporting documents and payment of a condonation fee. Students below 65 percent are barred from the examination and must repeat the course.

A medical exemption is available for absences caused by illness or hospitalisation. The student must submit a certificate from a registered medical practitioner within seven days of returning to class. Approved medical absence is counted as present for up to ten percent of the total classes held. Participation in university-approved sports and cultural events is treated as on-duty leave on the recommendation of the faculty coordinator.
