# Examination Rules (SAMPLE)

The minimum passing mark in a theory paper is 40 percent of the total marks, and students must also secure at least 40 percent in the combined internal and external components. Laboratory courses require a minimum of 50 percent.

Only a non-programmable scientific calculator is permitted in the examination hall, and only when the question paper explicitly allows it. Mobile phones, smart watches, and any written material are prohibited and must be deposited before entering the hall. Students must carry their admit card and university identity card at all times.

Any student found copying, exchanging material, or using unfair means will be reported to the Unfair Means Committee. Penalties range from cancellation of the paper to cancellation of the whole semester, depending on severity.

Students may apply for re-evaluation of an answer script within seven days of result publication by submitting the prescribed form to the Controller of Examinations and paying the re-evaluation fee per paper. The better of the original and revised marks is retained.
