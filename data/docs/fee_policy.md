# Fee Policy (SAMPLE)

Semester tuition fees must be paid before the last date announced in the academic calendar, which is usually fifteen days after the start of classes. Payment can be made online through the student portal or by demand draft at the accounts office.

A late fee of Rs. 100 per day applies after the deadline, up to a maximum of Rs. 2,000. Students who need a waiver of the late fee should write to the Registrar with the reason and supporting documents; the Registrar's office decides each application individually.

Merit scholarships are awarded to students who secure a CGPA of 9.0 or above in the previous academic year. Students with a CGPA between 8.5 and 9.0 receive a partial tuition waiver. Scholarship holders must maintain the required CGPA each year to continue receiving the benefit.
