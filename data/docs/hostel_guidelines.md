# Hostel Guidelines (SAMPLE)

Hostel rooms are allotted in the first two weeks of July for the new academic session, based on year of study and the date of application. Returning students must renew their allotment before the end of June.

The hostel gates close at 10:00 PM for first-year students and at 11:00 PM for senior students. Late entry requires prior written permission from the warden. Repeated violations may lead to cancellation of the hostel seat.

Visitors are permitted only in the visitors' lounge between 4:00 PM and 7:00 PM. Overnight stay of visitors in student rooms is not allowed under any circumstances.

A refundable security deposit of Rs. 5,000 is collected at the time of admission to the hostel and returned within thirty days of vacating the room, after deduction of any damage charges.
